Wedding Coffee Bar Guide for Central Florida Couples

A coffee or matcha bar gives guests a warm moment between the ceremony and the dance floor. This guide from Heavenly Roast Coffee, a veteran-owned mobile espresso and matcha cart, covers what couples in Orlando and Central Florida ask most.

1. Pick the right moment

Cocktail hour and the late-night window after dinner are the two most popular times. A 2–3 hour service covers both without crowding the bar. See our wedding coffee bar service.

3. Build a signature menu

Most couples choose two signature drinks named after themselves, plus classic lattes, cold brew and a non-coffee option like ceremonial-grade matcha. Browse the full coffee cart menu or read what ceremonial-grade matcha is.

4. Check venue logistics

Reserve a flat area about 8×8 ft near standard power. Outdoor Florida venues should plan shade for the cart in warmer months.

5. Book early

Fall and spring Saturdays fill first. Read how far in advance to book a coffee cart, then request your quote.
